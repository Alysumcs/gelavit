Frequently asked questions

How long does collagen take to work?
The first changes to skin, hair and nails are usually visible after 4–8 weeks of regular use. Joints and tendons take longer — we recommend a course of at least 3 months. Consistency matters: one missed dose changes nothing, a month off does.
Why fish collagen?
Fish (marine) collagen is predominantly type I — exactly the type that makes up skin, bones and tendons. It has smaller molecules and higher absorption than porcine or bovine collagen. Our collagen is clinically tested.
Do I need to take vitamin C separately?
No — vitamin C is already part of every Gelavit Pure® product. Without it, the body cannot build its own collagen from the amino acids you consume, so we treat it as a necessary part of the formula rather than an add-on.
Is Gelavit suitable with lactose intolerance or diabetes?
Yes. The products contain no lactose. We use sucralose as the sweetener, which does not affect glycaemia or raise blood sugar levels, making it suitable for diabetics.
How and when will my order arrive?
Orders received before 12:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day. Delivery within Slovakia usually takes 2–3 working days by post, or 24 hours by courier. Shipping is free on orders over €50.
Can I return a product?
Yes, within 14 days of receipt without giving a reason — provided the goods are unused and in the original packaging. Details are in our terms and conditions.
